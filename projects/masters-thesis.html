Acoustic Information for Multisensory Occupancy Detection using Machine Learning

This thesis explores the use of acoustic information combined with other sensor modalities for detecting room occupancy using machine learning techniques.

The research was conducted at Aalto University and investigates how sound patterns can be leveraged alongside traditional sensors to improve the accuracy of occupancy detection systems in smart buildings.
